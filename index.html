The pen and its patents

As the founding ML engineer and employee #1 at Vahini Technologies, I owned the AI, ML, data and software stack for an IMU-based handwriting pen. You write on ordinary paper, and the pen works out what you wrote from how it moved.

I built the machine learning pipeline end to end: sensor-data pipelines, noise reduction, and CNN and Bi-LSTM recognition models, then full-stack development and product integration. We ran pilots in 5 schools with more than 500 students. I also worked with Andhra Pradesh government stakeholders and served as liaison for our IIT Tirupati collaboration.

This work contributed to 2 granted Indian patents, covering handwriting recognition and structured digitization. I am the lead inventor on both.
